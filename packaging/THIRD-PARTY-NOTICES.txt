EndoscopeViewerWIN packages its own MIT-licensed source with Python, Tcl/Tk,
Pillow and the libraries collected by PyInstaller's Pillow hooks.
See the accompanying Python.txt, Pillow-and-dependencies.txt and Tcl/Tk notices.
The Python and Pillow notices also cover their bundled third-party components.

Built using PyInstaller (GPL with its executable-distribution exception):
https://pyinstaller.org/en/stable/license.html

The installer is generated with Inno Setup:
https://jrsoftware.org/isinfo.php

FFmpeg and ffplay are optional external applications; their binaries are not
included. Install them separately to enable MP4 recording / RTSP diagnostics.
They retain their respective licenses.
